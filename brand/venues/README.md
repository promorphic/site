# Venue logos

Official wordmarks, downloaded 2026-09-29 (Stellar on 2026-10-16) and stored byte for byte as published. Each venue has two files: `-on-light` is the owner's dark variant, shown in the light version of the site, and `-on-dark` is the owner's light variant, shown in the dark version. Do not edit, recolour or crop these files; replace them from the source when a brand kit updates.

| Venue | Source | `-on-light` | `-on-dark` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethereum | https://ethereum.org/assets/ | `ethereum-wordmark-black.svg` | `ethereum-wordmark-purple.svg` (no white wordmark is published) |
| Canton | https://www.canton.network/brand-kit-trademark-use | `canton-logo-black.svg` | `canton-logo-white.svg` |
| Stellar | https://stellar.org/brand-resources, `Stellar logo pack`, `Logo Press Kit 2026/Stellar/RGB/` (the network wordmark is published as PNG only; kept at 640px), downloaded 2026-10-16 | `Stellar Logo Final Black RGB.png` | `Stellar Logo Final White RGB.png` |
| Robinhood Chain | https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/brand-guidelines/, `robinhood-chain-brand-assets-v1.zip` | `Robinhood_Chain_Logo_Black.svg` | `Robinhood_Chain_Logo_White.svg` |

Each mark belongs to its owner and is used under that owner's brand terms. Check those terms before publishing: several restrict who may display the logo.
