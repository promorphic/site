Questions

Plain answers.

What is Promorphic?

Promorphic is agentic infrastructure for tokenized assets: a universal layer that lets AI agents discover, price, hold, move, redeem and pledge tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) across every issuer, venue and chain, through one connection.

What is a tokenized asset?

A tokenized asset is a real-world asset, such as a Treasury fund, a money-market fund, private credit or equity, issued as a token on a blockchain, so it can be held, transferred and settled onchain. Promorphic makes every tokenized asset look the same to an agent, whatever its issuer or chain.

What is agentic infrastructure?

Infrastructure built with software agents as the user. Every rule, price, eligibility check and venue is exposed in a form an agent can read, check and act on, under limits its owner sets, with every action recorded.

Which chains and tokenization platforms does Promorphic reach?

Tokenized assets on Ethereum, Solana, Canton, Arc, Robinhood Chain and Base, from issuers and platforms such as Securitize, Ondo, Superstate, Franklin Templeton, BlackRock BUIDL and Centrifuge. Naming a venue says where an agent can act; it does not imply a partnership.

How does Promorphic keep an agent from making a bad trade?

Every action is rehearsed against the venue’s own rules before it is sent, so nothing bounces and nothing goes through by accident. Every agent acts under a mandate its owner sets, one switch stops it, and every action is recorded.

Who is Promorphic for?

Agent platforms and wallets, treasuries and trading desks, asset issuers, and financial institutions on permissioned rails.

The assets are onchain. The agents are here.
