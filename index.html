The vision

One economy, seen from two sides.

Two economies are being built at once. The agentic economy: software that acts on behalf of people and companies. The on-chain economy: the world’s assets becoming programmable tokens.

Agents are the natural operators of tokenized assets, and tokenized assets are the natural balance sheet of agents. They are one economy seen from two sides. Promorphic is the connective tissue.

The problem

Agents can read tokenized markets. They cannot act in them without a human.

Every issuer and venue has its own rules, rhythms and rails, and the markets now run around the clock while the people who could click “confirm” do not.

01

Read-only

Agents can query tokenized assets almost everywhere and transact almost nowhere. Allocating a single dollar into a tokenized treasury still takes a human.

02

Fragmented

The same fund lives on six chains with six subscription paths, six sets of transfer rules, six NAV schedules and six redemption windows. Transfers fail unpredictably.

03

Always on

Markets run around the clock; operations run on business days. A Sunday 3am margin call on tokenized collateral needs someone awake, and that someone is software.

The universal layer

Connect once. Reach every tokenized asset.

Tokenized assets live across dozens of issuers and venues, each with its own rules, rhythms, and rails. Promorphic sits in front of all of them as one universal layer: an agent connects once, and can discover, price, hold, move, redeem, and pledge any tokenized asset, wherever it lives.

Ethereum, Solana, Canton, Arc, Robinhood Chain, Base, and beyond.

Stripe made every bank look the same to a developer. Promorphic makes every tokenized asset look the same to an agent, with correctness and guardrails built in.

What ships

Three things every agent needs before it touches a tokenized asset.

01

Universal access

Connect once, reach every tokenized asset. One normalized view of the market, and routing that picks the right venue, path and moment: mint or redeem versus secondary, best price, cutoff-aware.

02

Eligibility simulation

Before the agent acts, Promorphic dry-runs the action against the venue’s own rules: allowlists, transfer hooks, authorization, redemption windows. Nothing bounces, and nothing goes through by accident.

03

Truth layer

Corporate-action-aware pricing, NAV freshness, multiplier normalization, halt propagation, cross-chain supply and position reconciliation. Data an agent can trade on.

Guardrails ship with all three. Owner-set limits, allowed assets and counterparties, a kill switch, and a full action log.
